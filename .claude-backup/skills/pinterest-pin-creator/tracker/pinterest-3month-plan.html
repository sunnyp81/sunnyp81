Core Strategy

Posting Cadence

  • 12-13 pins per week (consistent daily posting)
  • 2 pins/day most days, 1 on weekends
  • Best times: 8-10pm UK (peak food browsing)
  • Batch create weekly, schedule via Activepieces

Content Mix (per month)

  • 15 roundup/list pins (collage layouts)
  • 20 "syns in X" product pins (hero/sidebar)
  • 10 guide/educational pins (filmstrip/diagonal)
  • 5 seasonal/trending pins (special layouts)

Board Distribution

  • Every pin goes to 1 primary + 1-2 secondary boards
  • synCount: all "syns in X" posts (biggest board)
  • Themed boards: dinner, breakfast, snacks, chicken
  • Rotate boards to keep all 10 active

Design Rules

  • Never repeat same layout for same article
  • Rotate through 7 layouts each week
  • 3D embossed headlines with character
  • Vary bg: purple, terracotta, sage, mustard, pink, red
