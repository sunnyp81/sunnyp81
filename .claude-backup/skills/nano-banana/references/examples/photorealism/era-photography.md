## Era Reference Table

| Era | Camera | Flash | Color | Grain | Artifacts |
|-----|--------|-------|-------|-------|-----------|
| 1980s | 110 film | harsh red-eye | warm, faded | heavy | light leaks |
| 1990s | disposable | harsh, flat | muted | visible | date stamps |
| Early 2000s | first digital | blown highlights | oversaturated | noise | low resolution |
| Late 2000s | point-and-shoot | auto with fill | accurate | minimal | slight blur |
| Polaroid | instant | fill flash | warm, uneven | unique | development marks |
