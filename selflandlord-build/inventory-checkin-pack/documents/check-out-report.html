Fair wear and tear cannot be charged for. This is the deterioration expected from reasonable use over the length of the tenancy (e.g. light carpet wear, minor scuffs). Deductions must reflect the property's age and condition at check-in, not a "betterment" upgrade. Support every deduction with the check-in inventory and dated photos.
