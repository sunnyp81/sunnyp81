# CV Tailoring Cheatsheet

One master CV (`Sunny-Patel-CV-master.md`); swap the deltas below per target. Rule: mirror the exact keywords in the job spec (modern ATS does semantic matching, but exact-term overlap still wins the first cut), keep one column, lead with quantified proof.

---

## Variant 1 — Head of SEO / GEO (employed, £80–100k)

- **Headline:** `Head of SEO & AI Visibility (AEO/GEO) | Programmatic SEO at Scale`
- **Profile tweak:** open with leadership: "...now **leading** AI-search visibility for a 13-client agency book and **running an SEO pod**."
- **Promote:** SEO Pod Leadership, strategy presentations, retention, team management. Move "Commercial & Leadership" skills up.
- **Bullets to foreground:** AEO-from-zero across 13 accounts · 60k impressions recovered · monthly strategy presentations · YouTube SEO new revenue line.
- **De-emphasise:** raw engineering depth (keep one Hermes line; don't let it read as IC-only).
- **Keywords to mirror if in spec:** AI Overviews, GEO, answer engines, topical authority, technical SEO strategy, team leadership, P&L/retention.

## Variant 2 — Contract / Interim (day-rate £700–900)

- **Headline:** `Interim Head of SEO / GEO Specialist — available [date] | Outside IR35`
- **Add a one-line availability + rate band** under contact: `Available for contract/interim · Outside IR35 · Remote or Reading/London`.
- **Profile tweak:** outcomes + speed: "ship recoveries and programmatic builds fast; no ramp time."
- **Foreground:** 7-day AI-Search Visibility Diagnostic, core-update recovery, programmatic builds, GitHub Actions pipelines (proof you deliver, not just advise).
- **Cut:** earlier-commercial-career detail down to one line. Contractors are bought on recent, relevant delivery.

## Variant 3 — Client capabilities one-pager (retainers £3–8k/mo)

This is **not** a CV. Structure:
- **Top:** name + `SEO & AI-Search Visibility Consultant` + sunnypatel.co.uk + Hello@.
- **Three offers, priced:** (1) AI-Search Visibility Diagnostic £2.5–4k · (2) GEO/AEO Retainer £3–8k/mo · (3) Programmatic SEO Build (project).
- **Proof band:** AEO across 13 accounts · 60k impressions recovered · 14,400-page builds · Hermes automation. **Capability-led, no client names** until delivered + written permission.
- **Frame:** "senior AI-search oversight + automation, without headcount" (the fractional pitch buyers search for).
- **Close:** book a fit-call CTA. Step-in-the-door diagnostic first for cold trust; qualify budget on call 1.

## Variant 4 — AI / Engineering-leaning role (AI-search product, automation)

- **Headline:** `AI Visibility Engineer | Autonomous SEO Agents | LLM Search Optimisation`
- **Move "Agentic Tooling & Engineering" above SEO experience.**
- **Foreground:** Hermes architecture, multi-agent orchestration, Claude Code daily use, Astro/Next/Workers, CI/CD pipelines, programmatic data pipelines.
- **Frame SEO as the domain**, agent-building as the craft.

## Per-application 5-minute checklist

1. Paste the job spec; pull its 8–12 hardest nouns/skills. Ensure each true one appears verbatim in your CV (skills line or a bullet).
2. Rewrite the **first profile sentence** to echo the role's headline phrase.
3. Reorder the top 3 Selected Achievements so the most spec-relevant is first.
4. Confirm: single column, no tables/icons/text-boxes, standard headers, .docx or text-PDF.
5. Proofread twice — 85% of recruiters have rejected on a single typo.
6. Attach the Hermes/AI-Overview Loom link. Skip the cover letter unless required (<20% are read pre-decision).

## Never (your own hard rules)

- No mentor name-drops in experience. No false/unearned creds. No "reading" filler items.
- No client names, logos, case studies, or discovery findings in public/marketing material before delivery + written permission.
- Never target Figment's client list. Never discount after a loss email.
