## Market reality (2026)

- **Head of SEO / GEO, UK:** typical £60–90k, top quartile £100k+; the best-paid listings explicitly want "AI search" ownership. Roles increasingly titled *Head of SEO/GEO*. (Experteer, IT Jobs Watch, Jooble)
- **Senior SEO contractor day rate:** median ~£500/day; senior/expert £80–150/hr. Inside-IR35 add 15–25% to gross. (IT Jobs Watch, YunoJuno, freelanceratecalculator.net)
- **Fractional SEO/AI lead:** $5–15k/mo (embedded tier ~25–40% of a full-time hire's loaded cost). The market is actively shifting from full-time hires to fractional senior oversight. (stridec.com, seosavages.com)
- **AEO/GEO retainers:** mid-market $2–8k/mo, enterprise $10–25k+/mo. Buyers hire a consultant when they have an execution team but need direction. (thedigitalelevator.com, firstpagesage.com)
- **Express Medicals proof:** your own Copilot-inbound lead already validated the £4.5k audit + £3.25k/mo shape. The demand is real and you've felt it.

**Watch-outs from your memory:** Figment has no non-compete/moonlighting ban, but a 3-month notice + garden-leave risk and a 9-month client non-solicit. Factor notice into start dates; never target Figment's client list.
